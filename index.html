PREVENT BEFORE YOU REPAIR

Maintenance Tips

Simple habits can help keep everyday items working longer.

01

Keep Devices Clean

Keep accessible vents free of dust and follow the manufacturer's cleaning instructions.

02

Use Suitable Chargers

Use compatible charging accessories and stop using cables that appear damaged.

03

Inspect Your Bicycle

Check tires, brakes, and visible loose parts before riding.

04

Read Product Manuals

Follow the recommended instructions for use, cleaning, and maintenance.

05

Store Items Properly

Keep items dry and protect them from excessive heat and avoidable damage.

06

Address Problems Early

Pay attention to unusual sounds or performance changes and seek professional help when needed.

What is FixIt?

FixIt is a student website prototype designed to organize basic repair guides, maintenance tips, and sample repair service categories in one accessible location.

The project demonstrates how a website can help users discover and access useful community information.
